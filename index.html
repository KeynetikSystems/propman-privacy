Privacy Policy for PropMan

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

PropMan ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use the PropMan mobile application.

1. Offline-First Local Data Storage

PropMan is engineered as an offline-first local application:

  • Property & Tenant Data: All property details, tenant rosters, unit IDs, phone numbers, email addresses, payment receipts, and meter readings are stored locally on your device in an encrypted database (256-bit AES SQLCipher). We do not collect, transmit, or store your tenant data on external servers.

2. Device Permissions & Usage

  • Camera & Photo Access: Camera access is used solely on-device to perform Optical Character Recognition (OCR) on water meter readings. Photos taken for OCR meter scanning are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to remote servers.
  • Contacts Access: Phonebook contact access is used exclusively on your device to let you pick contacts when managing tenant profiles. We do not upload, harvest, or share your contact list.

3. In-App Purchases

In-App Purchases for PropMan Pro are processed securely by the Google Play Store and Apple App Store. Your payment details are handled directly by Google/Apple and are never accessible to PropMan.

4. Data Backups & Exports

If you choose to export Excel reports, PDF invoices, or database backup archives, these files remain on your device or in your personal cloud storage (e.g. Google Drive or iCloud) under your direct control.
